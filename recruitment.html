About Recruitment

Who Can Join?

Recruitment for Epsilon Eta takes place at the beginning of every Fall semester. Membership is an opportunity to meet and create bonds with like-minded individuals who share the same passions. New members are expected to attend all new member events and programs, with Formal Initiation taking place prior to Thanksgiving Break.

Fall recruitment has wrapped up for this year, and dates for the next round will be announced here and on Instagram @epsilonetauofa. Want to hear about it first? Join our interest list below.

Eligibility

  • A career interest in aiding the environment
  • All majors are welcome!
